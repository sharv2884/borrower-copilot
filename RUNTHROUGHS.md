Essential expenses (rent): ₹28,000/mo · Age: 29 · Credit score: known, 780 ·
Property: no · Gold: no *(not asked — property already "no" was enough to
route her)*

income: ₹0 · Upcoming large expense: no
*(skipped: variable income share — not asked, since she's salaried)*

### Outputs
- **O1 Verdict — Borrow less.** She can be approved for more, but not safely for the full amount asked.
- **O2 Amount** — Lender may sanction up to **₹18,53,667**; her **safe** ceiling is **₹2,89,353**. Large gap: her existing EMI + rent already use up most of the room a responsible cap allows.
